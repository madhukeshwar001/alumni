## 📋 Executive Summary

**AlumNexus** is a **production-ready, frontend-only SaaS platform** for Tamil Nadu engineering institutions. The platform provides a centralized ecosystem for alumni engagement, mentorship, job opportunities, event management, and fundraising.

**Status:** Ready for immediate demonstration and deployment  
**Type:** Frontend-only (perfect for demos)  
**Audience:** University alumni networks, engineering institutions  
**Region:** Tamil Nadu, India
